About Loona AI

Loona AI · Loona Automation

Loona AI is a free AI chat assistant that lives in a single web page — no download, no account, and no settings screen standing between you and your first question.

One assistant, five kinds of help

The chat handles far more than small talk. As the homepage puts it: Chat · Tasks · Photos · Voice · Code. You can learn any topic explained simply, step by step; write and improve letters, posts and assignments; translate between English, Urdu and Roman Urdu; plan lessons, business ideas, routines and trips; and write, explain or fix code in any programming language. Attach a photo and Loona AI will look at it and answer questions about what it shows — a worksheet, a product, a signboard, a screenshot of an error. Replies come back in the language you used, so a question typed in Roman Urdu is answered in Roman Urdu.

Agent Tasks: ready-made instructions

Many people know what they want but not how to ask an AI for it. That is why Loona AI includes Agent Tasks: one tap loads a complete, ready-made instruction into the chat, in the style of the morning-planner and specialist agents on large agent platforms. You fill in the parts in [brackets], press send, and Loona AI does the work. It is the fastest route for repeated jobs — a daily plan, a lesson outline, a caption set — because the thinking about how to phrase the request has already been done for you.

How an answer is produced

When you press send, your message is transmitted to the Loona Engine, the automation workflow Loona Automation operates on Make.com (EU1), which passes it to an AI model and returns the reply to this page. Loona AI is not an offline tool and does not run inside your browser alone: it needs the internet, and the text of your conversation is processed by that engine in order to answer you. Nothing else is sent — as the site states, nothing goes anywhere except the engine that answers you.

Your chats stay on your device

Your chat list and conversation history are stored in this browser, on this device only — not in a Loona account, because there is no account. That also means the honest flip side: if you clear your browser data or switch phones, your old chats do not follow you. And because your messages are processed by the engine to generate replies, you should not paste passwords, banking details or private information about other people into the chat.

What Loona AI cannot do

It can be wrong, confidently — always double-check medical, legal, financial and factual answers with a qualified source before acting on them. It is not a doctor, lawyer or accountant. It does not have guaranteed live information about today's news, prices or weather, its photo understanding can misread poor-quality images, and this free version may occasionally be slow or unavailable when the engine is busy.

Who it is for

Students who want a topic explained at their level, teachers drafting material, shopkeepers and freelancers writing in a second language, parents helping with homework, and beginners who find large AI platforms intimidating. Loona AI was built to feel like asking a helpful person, not operating a machine.

Loona AI is part of Loona Automation, founded by Aurang Zeb Loona in Jaranwala, Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan, which builds free tools and AI services for everyday people and small businesses worldwide.
