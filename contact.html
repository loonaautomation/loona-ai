What to write to us about

Write to us about answers that were wrong or unhelpful, Agent Tasks you wish existed (a ready-made instruction for a job you repeat every week is exactly the kind of suggestion we act on), photo questions the assistant misread, translation problems between English, Urdu and Roman Urdu, or using Loona AI with your class, shop or team. If you are a teacher using Loona AI in a classroom, we especially want to hear what works and what does not.
