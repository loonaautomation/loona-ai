Loona AI — Frequently Asked Questions

Loona AI · Loona Automation

Is Loona AI free?

Yes. Loona AI is free to use — chat, Agent Tasks, photo questions, translation and code help included. The site is supported by Loona Automation and may show advertising, but the assistant itself costs nothing.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. There is no registration and no login. Your chats are stored in this browser on this device only, which is also why they do not transfer if you change phones or clear your browser data.

How does Loona AI produce its answers?

When you press send, your message goes to the Loona Engine, the automation workflow Loona Automation runs on Make.com (EU1), which passes it to an AI model and returns the reply to this page. Loona AI needs an internet connection and does not work offline.

What happens to what I type and the photos I attach?

Your message text (and a photo you attach, when you ask about it) is sent to the Loona Engine so it can be answered — nothing is sent anywhere else. Your conversation history itself stays in this browser. Even so, never paste passwords, banking details or private information about other people into the chat.

What can I ask Loona AI to do?

Learn any topic explained step by step, write and improve text, translate between English, Urdu and Roman Urdu, plan lessons, routines, trips and business ideas, get help with code in any language, ask questions about a photo, and run ready-made Agent Tasks that load a complete instruction with one tap.

What can Loona AI not do?

It cannot guarantee correct answers — it can be confidently wrong, so verify medical, legal, financial and factual answers with a qualified source. It has no guaranteed live access to today's news, prices or weather, it is not a doctor, lawyer or accountant, and it cannot send messages or act on your accounts.

Which languages does it reply in?

Loona AI replies in the language you write in: English, Urdu script or Roman Urdu.

What is the Loona Guide on the page?

The Guide is a small built-in helper that answers questions about Loona AI itself — what it does, its tasks, privacy and plans — instantly, without using the main engine. The main chat is where your real work gets done.
